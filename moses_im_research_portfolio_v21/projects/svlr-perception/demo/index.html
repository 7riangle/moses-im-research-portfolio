This brief demonstration shows the consent, volume check, headphone screening, practice, and two trial formats from my jsPsych coursework project on BROOD/BREWED perception.

No response data will be saved or transmitted. This is a public portfolio demonstration, not a research study. The seven-step vowel-duration continuum was part of the proposed design, not a completed validated stimulus set. This demo replays two representative placeholder recordings, with two main trials per context. The original PHP saving has been disabled. The original headphone pass requirement still applies.
